What's included

  • Clean black-and-white design that prints well

How to use this daily template

  1. Print the PDF. One sheet per day.
  2. Block fixed commitments first — meetings, meals, school pickup, commute.
  3. Add your top 2-3 priorities during your highest-energy window (usually 9-11am).
  4. Leave buffer time between blocks. 15-30 minutes is usually enough.
  5. Review at end of day — mark wins, move slipped tasks to tomorrow.

Who uses the daily template?

  • People who prefer visual structure — defined hour slots make time and transitions easier to see.
  • Deep-work professionals — protect 2-4 hour blocks for focus work.
  • Students — balance class, study, and breaks across a full day.
  • Remote workers — create structure without an office routine.
