Visual daily planner FAQ

Questions about planning a day in time blocks.

What is a visual daily planner?

A visual daily planner places tasks and calendar events on a timeline, so you can see what fits before the day begins. Align uses color-coded time blocks to show work, routines, meetings, free time, and overlaps in one schedule.

Is Align a calendar planner or a to-do list?

Align combines both ideas. It brings events from your iPhone calendars into the same visual schedule as your time blocks, so you can decide when tasks happen instead of keeping a separate list.

What happens when my schedule changes?

You can drag or resize a time block as plans change. With Overlap Avoidance in Align Plus, later blocks can shift out of the way while keeping their original duration, helping the rest of the day stay realistic.

Can a visual planner help with ADHD time management?

People who prefer visual structure may find it easier to estimate time and switch tasks when the day is shown as concrete blocks. Align is a planning tool, not a medical treatment, and its main benefit is making time visible without adding a complicated workflow.

Is Align free?

Align is free to download on iPhone. Core features include unlimited time blocks, a visual timeline, calendar sync, recurring blocks, reminders, colors, icons, and drag-and-drop planning. Optional Align Plus features are available as in-app purchases.
