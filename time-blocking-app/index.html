See your day as a schedule, not another list

A to-do list tells you what needs attention. A time blocking app shows when each task can happen, how long it will take, and what it competes with. Align places time blocks, free-time gaps, and events from your iPhone calendars on the same color-coded timeline.

What happens when the plan changes?

Move or resize the block that changed instead of rebuilding the entire day. With Overlap Avoidance in Align Plus, later blocks can shift out of the way while keeping their original duration. You can see the proposed timing on the timeline before saving it.

That flexibility is the difference between a schedule you abandon and one you can keep using.

A visual planner for focus and time management

Visual blocks make time concrete. You can color-code types of work, see transition gaps, set reminders, repeat routines, and check off completed blocks. People who struggle with time estimation or task switching may find this clearer than an open-ended task list, without turning the product into a complicated all-in-one system.

Free features and Align Plus

  • Unlimited time blocks on a visual timeline
  • Calendar sync through the iPhone calendar system
  • Recurring blocks, reminders, colors, and icons
  • Completion tracking and drag-and-drop reorganization

Align Plus adds advanced controls, including overlap avoidance, subtasks, time-block locking, and custom day start and end times. Prices are shown in the App Store before purchase.

How to build a realistic time-blocked day

  1. Bring in fixed commitments. Choose the device calendars you want to see, then start with meetings, appointments, meals, and other fixed events.
  2. Block the work that matters. Give the most important tasks a start time and a realistic duration.
  3. Leave visible breathing room. Keep transitions and free-time gaps instead of filling every minute.
  4. Adjust when the day changes. Drag or resize the block that moved, review the new timing, and continue from the updated plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is a time blocking app?

A time blocking app turns tasks into scheduled blocks with a start time, end time, and visible place in the day. Align shows those blocks beside calendar events on one iPhone timeline.

Is Align free?

Yes. Align is free to download, with optional Align Plus tools available as in-app purchases.

Does Align work with Google Calendar?

Yes, through the iPhone calendar system. Add your Google account in iOS Settings, then choose that calendar inside Align.

Is Align an ADHD planner?

Align is a visual planning tool rather than an ADHD treatment. People who prefer a concrete timeline may find time estimation and task switching easier when the day is shown in blocks.

Which devices support Align?

Align is currently listed on the App Store as an iPhone app.
