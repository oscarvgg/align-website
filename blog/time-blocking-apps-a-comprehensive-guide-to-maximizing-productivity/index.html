Best Time Blocking Apps for iPhone in 2026 — Free & Paid

Updated on Sep 11, 2026 · Published on Aug 18, 2023
  • Apps

written by Oscar Gonzalez

Time blocking means giving every task a place on the clock instead of leaving it on an open-ended list. It works, but doing it on paper falls apart the moment the day moves — which is most days. A dedicated time blocking app or digital planner earns its place by making the replan cheap. If you want the technique itself first, start with our introduction to time blocking, or grab a free template if paper still suits you.

Here is how the main iPhone options actually compare.

Disclosure and method. Align publishes this comparison, so treat our own entry with the scepticism it deserves — we have tried to earn it by naming the cases where another app is the better choice. Everything in the table below was checked on 11 September 2026 against each app's public App Store listing and its official website. These are documented features as the vendors describe them, not hands-on test results; we have not run a controlled trial of every app. Subscription prices change often and are set per storefront, so we link to each vendor's pricing rather than quoting a number that will be stale within weeks.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Structured if you want the same plan on your Mac, Apple Watch and the web, or if you want missed tasks pushed forward for you automatically. It is the most established app in this list by a wide margin, and it includes a Pomodoro timer, which Align does not have. The trade-off: connecting your calendar and building recurring routines both require Pro.

Pick Sorted³ if you dislike subscriptions. Pro is a one-time purchase, which over a few years is the cheapest route here, and Auto Schedule is genuinely good at filling in a day you have not planned. It also covers iPhone, iPad, Watch and Mac.

Pick TimeBloc if you need Android, or you are switching between an iPhone and an Android device. Neither Align, Structured, Sorted³ nor Todoist can help you there.

Pick Todoist if what you actually need is a task manager rather than a time blocker — shared projects, assignees, labels, filters across a lot of work. It has a calendar view, but it is not built to plan a day on a timeline, and the free plan caps you at five personal projects.

Pick Align if your problem is specifically the replan: you build a sensible day, something runs long, and you want to fix the rest of the day in a few seconds rather than rebuild it. Calendar sync and recurring blocks are in the free tier rather than behind the paywall, which is the clearest difference between Align and Structured on price. Align Plus adds Insights — a read-back of how the day actually went — plus subtasks, block locking, and surrounding blocks that move out of the way when you drag or resize one.

Do not pick Align if you need a Mac, Apple Watch or web version, an Android app, a built-in Pomodoro timer, or team features. Those are real gaps today, not oversights we are glossing over.

The apps in more detail

  1. Align — a visual time blocking app for iPhone that puts your whole day on one timeline. Create unlimited blocks, colour and label them, set reminders, and sync the calendars already on your device, all in the free tier. Recurring blocks keep a routine in place without rebuilding it each morning, and Home and Lock Screen widgets show what is next. Align Plus adds Insights, weekly and monthly review, streaks, subtasks, time block locking, custom day boundaries, and overlap avoidance that moves surrounding blocks out of the way when you drag or resize one.

  2. Structured — the most widely used app here, and the most complete on platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and web, all syncing. The free tier gives you the visual timeline, drag-and-drop, subtasks with notes, a Pomodoro timer, Live Activities and interactive widgets. Structured Pro adds calendar and Reminders integration, recurring tasks and routines, and Replan, which automatically reschedules tasks you missed.

  3. Sorted³ — merges calendar events and tasks into a single timeline, with natural-language input and iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac. Its Auto Schedule feature works out a realistic running order for what is left of your day. Notable for its pricing model: Pro is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, with iOS and macOS sold separately or bundled.

  4. TimeBloc — blocks your day by the hour or minute, with icons, coloured tags and dark mode, and it is the only app in this list with an Android version. Calendar integration is available, though unlimited calendars sit behind TimeBloc Premium, a subscription that starts after a free trial.

  5. Todoist — not a time blocking app, but it belongs here because many people try to use it as one. It is an excellent task manager with recurring tasks, natural-language capture, shared projects and assignees, and it runs essentially everywhere. The free Beginner plan allows 5 personal projects, 3 filter views, one week of activity history and 5 collaborators per project.

Benefits of Time Blocking Apps

Time blocking apps offer several advantages that can greatly enhance productivity and time management.

1. Improve Focus

By allocating specific time slots for tasks in your day, you can prioritize your work and stay focused on the task at hand. Time blocking apps help eliminate distractions by keeping you focused on designated activities.

2. Improved Productivity

One of the main benefits of time blocking apps is their ability to boost productivity. By allocating specific time blocks for tasks, individuals can prioritize their work efficiently. These apps help eliminate distractions by keeping users focused on their designated activities. With this laser-like focus, productivity increases significantly, leading to higher quality outputs and faster completion of tasks.

3. Enhanced Time Management

Time blocking apps allow users to create a well-structured schedule, ensuring optimal utilization of time. By allocating specific time slots for each task, individuals can avoid procrastination and stay on track. These apps help break down larger projects into smaller, manageable blocks, making them more approachable. With a clear schedule in place, individuals can easily see their progress, meet deadlines, and maintain a sense of control over their time.

4. Increased Accountability

One crucial aspect of time blocking is accountability, and time blocking apps excel in this area. These apps enable users to track their progress in real-time and stay committed to the allocated time blocks. By seeing how their time is allocated and measuring their achievements, individuals become more self-disciplined and accountable for their time management.

5. Reduced Stress

One of the main reasons people feel overwhelmed is because they don't have control over their time. Time blocking apps help you regain control and remove the stress of managing your schedule. You can easily plan your day, knowing that you have allocated sufficient time to complete your tasks.

Key features to look for in Time Blocking Apps

When choosing a time blocking app, it's important to consider key features that suit your needs.

  1. Calendar Integration: A crucial feature to look for in time blocking apps is calendar integration. These apps allow you to plan your day and seamlessly sync with existing calendars. This integration prevents double bookings and overlaps in your schedule. Check whether it is in the free tier — in some apps it is not.
  2. Task Management: Task management capabilities are essential in time blocking apps. Apps that allow you to create and categorize tasks within your time blocks are more efficient. Additionally, features like reminders and notifications for upcoming tasks ensure you never miss a beat and stay on top of your schedule.
  3. Flexibility and Customization: Flexibility and customization options are valuable when choosing a time blocking app. Each individual has their own work style and preferences, so finding an app that can tailor time blocks to accommodate these needs is crucial. Look for apps that allow you to adjust and rearrange your schedules as needed, ensuring that your time blocking approach is personalized and effective.
  4. Synchronization with multiple devices: If you plan on more than one device, check the platform row in the table above before you commit. This is the single biggest difference between the apps listed here.

Conclusion

The honest summary is that there is no single best time blocking app, only the one that matches how your days break. If you need the plan everywhere, Structured. If you refuse to rent software, Sorted³. If you need Android, TimeBloc. If you need a task manager more than a timeline, Todoist. If the thing that defeats you is rebuilding the day after it slips, that is the case Align is built for, and calendar sync is free.

Whichever you choose, the app matters less than the habit. Block tomorrow before you finish today, keep the blocks honest about how long things take, and expect to move them.
